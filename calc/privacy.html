A-1.9Privacy policy

Housewright Calc

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Housewright Calc is built to work entirely on your device. Our privacy policy is short because there is almost nothing to tell.

What we collect

Nothing. Housewright Calc has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections of its own. Your calculations, saved history, and settings live only on your device (and your own device backups, if you use iCloud or iTunes backup).

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us aggregate sales numbers only — never your identity. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID by Apple, which is what lets you restore it on any of your devices.

Cut sheets and sharing

When you export a cut-sheet PDF or share a calculation, the file goes only where you send it, using the standard iOS share sheet. We never receive a copy.

Data we hold about you

None. There is no server. If you delete the app, everything it stored is gone (except your own backups).

Children

Housewright Calc does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything (for example, optional crash reporting), we will update this policy first and the app will ask you before enabling it.

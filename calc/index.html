A-1.1Exact math

Feet, inches and fractions, the way you say them.

Type 4' 3-3/4" + 5' 6-3/16" and get 9' 9-15/16". It is the exact answer, held as a fraction the whole way through, never a decimal that got rounded somewhere in the middle. People cut lumber from these numbers.

A-1.7Measure twice

A good tool still wants a second look.

Housewright Calc is a tool in a professional's hands, and the professional is still you. Before you order or cut, check every quantity, cut list and material list against your own plans. Before you build, check every code reference against the rules where you build: your local code and your inspector have the last word, not an app.

We work hard to get the math right, and we fix what we get wrong. Even so, Calc is provided as it is. Housewright can't accept responsibility for loss, damage, delay or lost business that comes from relying on its results, including a result that turns out to be wrong. Its answers are yours to confirm, the way you'd read a tape twice before the saw comes down.

A-1.8Support

Questions, answered by the person who built it.

Write to housewrightapp@gmail.com.

Step-by-step guides for every calculator, with screenshots: Help & guides. The same pages are inside the app, and work with no signal.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes, all of it. Calc makes no network connections of its own. Voice entry is recognised on the phone, and cut lists you send are generated on the phone and shared by you.

What do you collect?

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads. The privacy policy is short because there is almost nothing to tell.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Tap Restore on the Pro purchase screen. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, and Family Sharing is on.

Which laser measures work?

Bluetooth Bosch laser measures: the GLM 50 C and the PLR C-series. Pair once in Settings and each reading lands in the calculator as an exact fraction.

How far should I trust the code checks?

They cite the IRC 2021 section they come from and tell you to verify local amendments, because your jurisdiction's rules win. Header, beam and joist results carry engineer-verification language on purpose. The rest is under Measure twice.

Is it on iPad?

Yes. The same purchase covers iPhone and iPad.
